Get the celebrity treatment with world-class service at Four Seasons Hotel Austin

Located in the heart of downtown Austin, Texas, this hotel offers 294 spacious guest rooms with views of the city centre or Lady Bird Lake.

A large flat-screen TV, work desk and lounge chair are included in each warmly-decorated room at Four Seasons Hotel Austin. Complimentary WiFi is available in every room.

A heated saltwater pool is located on the property. Guests can work up a sweat in the state-of-the-art fitness centre enjoy a relaxing spa treatment in the award-winning Spa at Four Seasons Hotel Austin. The on-site 24-hour concierge can assist with business, entertainment or travel arrangements. Two self-service business centres are available for guest use.

The onsite restaurant, Ciclo, is located on the property's lower level overlooking Lady Bird Lake. It  features a menu of locally-inspired flavors and ingredients with a Latin influence. The restaurant is open for breakfast, lunch and dinner daily.

Several key attractions, including the Texas State Capitol, Zilker Park and 6th Street entertainment district are located within 3.5 km of the Four Seasons.The University of Texas at Austin is less than 10 minutes’ drive from this hotel.

Couples in particular like the location – they rated it 8.5 for a two-person trip.
