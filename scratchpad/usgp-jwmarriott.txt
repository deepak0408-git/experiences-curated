Located in the heart of downtown Austin, JW Marriott features a luxurious, modern design throughout the property. Free Wifi for Marriott Bonvoy Members and a high-definition TV are provided in each room. Dine in the 3 elegant on-site restaurants or relax at the rooftop pool overlooking the city streets.

Crisp white linens and plush bedding are included in each room. Free toiletries are available in an en suite bathroom.  Ironing facilities, a desk and a coffee machine are also provided.

An on-site Starbucks, fully equipped fitness center and spa are all located at the JW Marriott. Luggage storage and a 24-hour front desk are also provided.

Austin-Bergstrom International Airport is 15 minutes’ drive from this hotel.  The Capitol Building is located 1 km away from Austin’s JW Marriott.

Couples in particular like the location – they rated it 9.4 for a two-person trip.
