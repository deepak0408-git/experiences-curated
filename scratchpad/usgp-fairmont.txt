Fairmont Austin offers accommodation located 100 metres from Austin Convention Center. This property is located a short distance from Capitol Building and Frank Erwin Center - University of Texas. The property provides lake views, a sun terrace, a fitness centre, an outdoor swimming pool and a 24-hour front desk.

The rooms at the hotel are fitted with a seating area, a flat-screen TV with cable channels and a private bathroom with free toiletries and a bath or shower. Fairmont Austin features some units with city views, and rooms are fitted with a coffee machine. All rooms at the accommodation feature air conditioning and a desk.

Several dining options are available on property including Fulton, Rules & Regs, Good Things and In-Room Dining.

In the wellness area you will find a hot tub and a sauna.

Texas Memorial Stadium is 2.5 km from Fairmont Austin, while Barton Springs Pool is 2.9 km away. The nearest airport is Austin-Bergstrom International Airport, 9 km from the property.

Couples in particular like the location – they rated it 9.1 for a two-person trip.
