Offering a year-round outdoor pool and fitness centre, South Congress Hotel is a boutique hotel located in Austin's South Congress District. The hotel has a terrace and sun terrace, and guests can enjoy a meal at the restaurant. Private parking is available on site.

Each room at this hotel is air conditioned, has a flat-screen TV and offers a private bathroom. Some units have a seating area where you can relax while other rooms offer views of the pool, garden or city. For your comfort, you will find bath robes and free toiletries in each room. South Congress Hotel features free WiFi throughout the property.

You will find a 24-hour front desk, a cash machine, gift shop and shops at the property.

San Marcos is 45 km from South Congress Hotel, while Round Rock is 30 km from the property. The nearest airport is Bergstrom Airport, 10 km from the property.

Couples in particular like the location – they rated it 9.6 for a two-person trip.
