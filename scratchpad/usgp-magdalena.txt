About this property

Comfortable Accommodations: Hotel Magdalena in Austin offers air-conditioned rooms with private bathrooms, minibars, and free WiFi. Each room includes a coffee machine, TV, and hairdryer.

Dining and Leisure: Guests can enjoy a modern restaurant serving American cuisine, a bar, and a year-round outdoor swimming pool. Additional facilities include a terrace, balcony, and seating area.

Convenient Location: Located about 5 miles from Austin-Bergstrom International Airport, the hotel is near attractions such as Shoal Beach (an 18-minute walk) and the Austin Convention Center (about 1.2 miles). Boating is available in the surroundings.

Guest Services: The hotel provides a 24-hour front desk, concierge service, room service, and breakfast in the room. Paid on-site private parking is available.

Couples in particular like the location – they rated it 9.7 for a two-person trip.
