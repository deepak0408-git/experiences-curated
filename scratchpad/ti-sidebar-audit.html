Four real failure modes

  1. sports_venue false negatives (Las Vegas ×3, Singapore ×3): real grandstands typed sports_venue instead of fan_experience. T1 misses these entirely; only T2 catches them, and only when that event's spoke table entry is complete.
  2. fan_experience false positives with no T2 entry to correct them: Abu Dhabi (Skybridge Terrace, Yacht Charter — worse, also mis-tagged luxury), Australian GP (Lakeside Festival), Belgian & Hungarian GP (classic format, zero T2 coverage), Mexico City (Fan Zone), Chinese GP (4 non-track rows, no table entry exists).
  3. T2 table incompleteness: Italian GP's "Trackside Corner Lounges & Villas", Mexico City missing 5 of 21 entries, Chinese GP missing entirely — the curator table lags behind real seeded experiences, a structural staleness risk for any filter that leans on it.
  4. Classic-format events get zero T2 coverage by design (Belgian GP, Hungarian GP) — EXPERIENCE_TO_SPOKE_BY_EVENT is hub-and-spoke only, so T1-only false positives on these two live events are unresolvable without a third signal.

Neither signal alone is reliable, and combined (T1 OR T2), real false positives survive on at least 5 events (Abu Dhabi, Australian GP, Belgian GP, Mexico City, Chinese GP), plus a false-negative class (sports_venue) that only T2 saves — and T2 itself isn't complete or current. A title/slug keyword check (matching "Grandstand", "Paddock Club", "General Admission", "Ticket Guide", "Hospitality", "GA") would likely close most remaining gaps, but carries its own fragility — worth a deliberate decision rather than a default.
