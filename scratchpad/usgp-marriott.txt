Located near Lady Bird Lake, Austin Marriott Downtown provides accommodation with an outdoor swimming pool, private parking, a terrace and a restaurant. This 4-star hotel offers a 24-hour front desk, an ATM and free WiFi. The property is 2.4 km from Festival Beach, and within less than 1 km of the city centre.

The hotel offers a continental or American breakfast.

Popular points of interest near Austin Marriott Downtown include Austin Convention Center, Capitol Building and Congress Avenue Bridge. The nearest airport is Austin-Bergstrom International Airport, 10 km from the accommodation.

Couples in particular like the location – they rated it 9.5 for a two-person trip.
