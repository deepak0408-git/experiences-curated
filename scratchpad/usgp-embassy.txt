About this property

Comfortable Accommodations: Embassy Suites by Hilton Austin Downtown South Congress in Austin offers family rooms with air-conditioning, free toiletries, and modern amenities. Each room includes a work desk, refrigerator, and TV.

Dining and Leisure: Guests can enjoy an à la carte breakfast, lunch, and dinner at the family-friendly restaurant serving American cuisine. The bar offers cocktails in a traditional and modern ambience. Additional facilities include a fitness centre, indoor swimming pool, and lounge.

Prime Location: Located less than 1 km from Shoal Beach and near attractions such as the Capitol Building (2 km) and Austin Convention Center (13-minute walk). Austin-Bergstrom International Airport is 11 km away. Boating is available in the surroundings.

Couples in particular like the location – they rated it 9.0 for a two-person trip.
